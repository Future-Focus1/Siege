# SIEGE

Portrait siege game. You build a keep out of loose blocks, then knock the other keep down with a catapult. Pieces are real physics bodies. They crack, slip, and fall. Nothing plays a canned collapse.

## What you can do

- Pull the catapult with a finger or the mouse. The arc you see is the arc the stone flies.
- Quick Battle throws heavy boulders at a timber outpost until the keep core breaks or falls.
- The tower builder spends a budget of 100 on wood, stone, and metal. You can rotate, remove, undo, redo, save, and test.
- Single player has five keeps. Easy, Normal, and Hard change how the defender aims and which weapon it picks. They do not add health.
- A loadout spends a separate budget of 12 on weapons and one perk.
- Sandbox is the builder plus a firing range. It is on the main menu.
- The multiplayer screen does not search for players. It can run a local two-sided battle on this device.

## How a fight works

You only see one keep at a time. Your shot looks at their keep, with the catapult toward the lower left and the tower on the right. Their shot looks at your keep. Both keeps stay in the state the last impact left them.

A shared clock decides who shoots. A fast weapon is ready sooner than a heavy one. The clock waits while someone is aiming. After the shot, the game lets the rubble settle, then moves on.

The fight ends when a keep core is destroyed, when that core is knocked far from where it was built, or when the clock passes the battle limit. If time runs out, the steadier keep wins. Equal keeps tie.

## What is not in the game

Online matches, accounts, and real-money upgrades are not in this build. Extra weapons and defences from the brief are data slots for later, not things you can equip today.
